# Concept notes — v3 "Measured from Zero Mile"

The full research and creative thesis are in `docs/RESEARCH.md`.

## Why v3
v1 and v2 were competent but generic: either a typographic brochure or a portfolio grid that any studio could have. v3 starts from the research question "what is true of this practice and of no other?" The answer is its client list. Read in order, it is Vidarbha's working chain, from market to mill to school to city to home. Each public client is a town at a measurable distance from Nagpur's Zero Mile.

## Pages, sections and interactions
**Home**
1. **Hero (third revision).** A giant coral नागपूर sits behind the practice's own Manjusha Regalia building (sky cut out of the rendering), with Nagpur's coordinates, "Practice since 1980", the headline "Market yards to family homes, designed in Nagpur.", two calls to action and a floating APMC case-study card. On phones the stack is: word, building, text, card.
2. **Sticky chain bar.** बाजार Market → गिरणी Mill → शाळा School → शहर City → घर Home, highlighting the chapter in view.
3. **Market.** The godowns rendering with its real title block, shed and auditorium, and a case-study link.
4. **Mill.** A cotton-chain diagram (कापूस → जिनिंग → प्रेसिंग → सूतगिरणी) placing the two co-operative clients on it, with an honest note that no drawings are published.
5. **School.** A ledger of institutional clients with the hostel, training-centre and Konark Chambers renderings.
6. **City.** The Ushakaal (उषःकाल, "dawn") series as a numbered strip of eight, the Pearl Heritage and Nostalgia townships, and the 14 developers.
7. **Home.** A scrolling rail of 15 anonymised bungalows plus an interiors mosaic (photographs).
8. **Index.** A numbered catalogue of all 63 items with chapter and medium, filters and a lightbox.
9. **Practice.** Portraits, credentials and the three services.
10. **Enquiry.** "Where on the chain is your project?" — the five chain links are the project types, plus a project-management option, validation and a local-only confirmation.

**Case study:** rebuilt in the v3 language (बाजार chapter).

## How it improves on the current site
- **Positioning.** "Architect in Nagpur" with stock photos becomes a specific claim backed by named towns and drawings.
- **Discovery.** The work is findable by the client's own situation (market, factory, institution, housing, home), not by a service menu.
- **Trust.** Real title blocks, labelled renderings, credentials, and no unverifiable testimonials.
- **Local fit.** Bilingual Marathi and English for a Marathi-speaking public and institutional client base.
